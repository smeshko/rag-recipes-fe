Spinach & Cheddar Frittata

All the awesomeness of an omelet without the folding — started in a skillet on the stovetop, then baked in the oven until puffy.

Serves 4–6
Total 30 minutes
One oven-safe skillet

Ingredients

7 items · tap to check off
2 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil
1 small onion, peeled and diced
1 bag (6 oz) baby spinach
8 large eggs
1 tsp salt
½ tsp black pepper
8 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded

Method

7 steps · extracted with 0.99 confidence
1.

Preheat the oven to 375°F.

2.

Add the olive oil to your skillet and heat over medium. Once the oil begins to shimmer, add the onion. Sauté until translucent, about 6–8 minutes.

3.

Add half the spinach and stir it into the onion — it will wilt quickly. Add the other half and wilt once again, 3–4 minutes.

4.

Lower the heat to medium-low. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, salt, pepper and cheese. Immediately pour the mixture into the skillet and gently stir with a rubber spatula to incorporate the spinach and onion.

5.

Leave the skillet over medium-low heat until small bubbles come to the surface regularly, about 6–8 minutes.

This is how you'll know the bottom crust is forming.

6.

Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake for 10 minutes, or until the frittata gets "puffy." It will seem to deflate once removed — that's expected.

7.

Let it cool to room temperature. Serve directly from the skillet, or shake to release the bottom and slide onto a plate for cutting.
